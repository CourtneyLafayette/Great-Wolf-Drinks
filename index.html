Fiery Mango Margarita

Sweet mango. Bright citrus. A slow jalapeño kick.

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz mango purée
  • 3/4 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1/2 oz orange liqueur
  • 1/2 oz agave nectar
  • 2–3 fresh jalapeño slices
  • Tajín, for the rim
  • Ice

Build

  1. Rim a rocks glass with Tajín and fill the glass with fresh ice.
  2. Add the jalapeño slices and agave nectar to a shaker and gently muddle.
  3. Add tequila, mango purée, fresh lime juice, orange liqueur, and ice.
  4. Shake hard until well chilled.
  5. Double strain over fresh ice in the prepared glass.
  6. Finish with the approved garnish and serve immediately.
GarnishLime wheel + jalapeño slice
GlasswareRocks glass
RimTajín
