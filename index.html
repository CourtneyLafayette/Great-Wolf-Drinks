Fiery Mango

MARGARITA

A bold and refreshing margarita with sweet mango, a touch of heat, and a smooth citrus finish.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 oz premium silver tequila
  • 1 oz mango purée
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 0.5 oz triple sec or orange liqueur
  • 0.5 oz agave nectar or simple syrup
  • 2–3 slices fresh jalapeño

BUILD

  1. Muddle jalapeño slices in a shaker to release flavor.
  2. Add tequila, mango purée, lime juice, triple sec, agave, and ice.
  3. Shake well until chilled.
  4. Strain into a rocks glass filled with fresh ice.
  5. Finish with garnish and serve.
GLASSWARERocks glass over fresh ice
RIMTajín or chili-salt rim
GARNISHMango slice, dehydrated lime wheel & fresh jalapeño slice
SERVICE STANDARDBright, cold, balanced and clean presentation
